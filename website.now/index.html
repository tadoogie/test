Welcome to the Digital Splitleaf!

This is a new kind of metrical psalter, inspired by the traditional split-leaf format where tunes and texts could be freely matched. Now, you can do that quickly and easily with the help of digital technology.

Select your psalm text and tune, and the Digital Splitleaf will automatically underlay the text beneath the music, making it simple to read and sing. Whether you are leading worship, singing in a group, or practicing on your own, the interface is designed to support your singing experience.

We are building a community of psalm singers who are passionate about this rich musical practice, committed to singing the psalms today and promoting them for future generations. We invite you to explore, sing, and share.
