Community

The Digital Splitleaf is supported by a community of users who are committed to promoting and preserving historical and current psalm-singing practice. If you are interested in ensuring that psalms continue to be widely available to future generations, consider joining the community.

GitHub Contributors

The workshops (see Events, here) are designed to provide basic training for contributing to the community. More information can also be found on the Digital Splitleaf on GitHub.

Blog
